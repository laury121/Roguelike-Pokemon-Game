Title: Choose Your Own

Assignment Description:
For this final assignment it will be something of your choosing. It should be similar in complexity as the previous assignments this semester. 

My Project:
For this assignment, I implemented use of the buildings. For the Pokemon Center, there is an interactive screen where you can choose to heal your party for free, view all the pokemon in your party, or switch who your lead is for when you enter battles. For the PokeMart, you can buy pokeballs, healing potions, and revives. It also will show you your inventory and how much money you currently have. I also added a Bank building where you can see how much money you have and then play minigames to get more money to use in the PokeMart. 
Besides buildings, I changed the pokeball catch rate from 1 to a random chance number that is influenced by pokemon hp (lower hp is easier to catch). If there is room in your party, then the pokemon will get added. If not, then nothing happens to the pokemon.
